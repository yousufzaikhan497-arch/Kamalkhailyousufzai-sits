Our Full History

From our earliest recorded roots to how our family came to Shangla — and how we've scattered across the world since.

زموږ له لومړني نسب څخه تر شنګلې پورې — او دا چې زموږ کورنۍ څنګه نړۍ ته خواره شوه.

Origins

From Kandahar

Our tribe, the Yousafzai, is part of the wider Sarbani confederation of Pashtun tribes. According to the earliest accounts, our ancestors originally lived in the countryside around Kandahar in southern Afghanistan. There, land was traditionally divided among kinship groups by lot — a custom that shaped how the tribe organized itself for generations.

A dispute over land and water rights with a neighboring tribe eventually pushed our ancestors out of the Kandahar region. Kinsmen granted them a new stretch of land near the Tarnak river, but this was only the beginning of a much longer journey east.

زموږ قبیله، یوسفزی، د پښتنو د سربني اتحاد یوه برخه ده. زموږ نیکونه اصلاً د قندهار سیمې کلي وو، چیرې چې ځمکه د خپلوۍ ډلو ترمنځ په پچه ویشل کیده. د اوبو او ځمکې پر سر شخړه وروسته زموږ نیکونه دې سیمې پریښودلو ته اړ کړل.

Migration

The Journey Through Kabul

As the tribe moved eastward, they passed through the Kabul area during a period when it was ruled by a Timurid governor. Historical accounts describe a period of serious tension between Yousafzai leaders and the ruling authorities of the time, which pushed the tribe to continue moving rather than settle permanently in that region.

The tribe eventually reached the mouth of the Khyber Pass in the early 16th century, under the leadership of Malik Ahmed. From there, they moved into the Peshawar valley — land that, at the time, was already home to other Pashtun groups, including the Dilazak.

کله چې قبیله ختیځ ته روانه شوه، د کابل له لارې تیره شوه. زموږ نیکونه د خیبر درې خولې ته ورسیدل، او د ملک احمد په مشرۍ د پیښور درې ته ننوتل، چې هلته وړاندې د دلازک پښتانه اوسیدل.

Settlement

Peshawar and Swat

Settling in Peshawar required negotiation — and eventually conflict — with the Dilazak who already occupied the valley. Over time, relations between the Yousafzai and the local Dilazak broke down, and a prolonged struggle followed. The Yousafzai ultimately prevailed and expanded their territory further into the Swat valley and surrounding regions.

This period marked the tribe's transformation from newcomers into the dominant power across a wide stretch of what is today northern Khyber Pakhtunkhwa — including Swat, Buner, Mardan, Swabi, Dir, Malakand, and Shangla, our own homeland.

د پیښور په درو کې میشتیدل د دلازکو سره جګړه ته اړتیا درلوده. یوسفزیو وروسته بریالیتوب ترلاسه کړ او خپله سیمه یې سوات، بونیر، مردان، سوابۍ، دیر، مالاکنډ او شنګلې ته پراخه کړه — زموږ خپل ټاټوبی.

Our Branch

Akozai, Baizai, and Kamal Khel

As the Yousafzai settled and grew, the tribe divided into major branches, each descended from a different son or grandson. Our own line traces through Akozai — one of these major branches — and within Akozai, through Baizai. It is from Baizai that our branch, Kamal Khel, descends, named after our ancestor Kamal Khan, whose grave still stands today in Shangla, Besham Kerai.

This is how our confirmed line runs: Khan Usman → Charabi Khan → Kamal Khan → Muladaray Khan → Mohammed Salim → Mohammed Aurang → Samiullah Yousafzai — a respected leader who studied Islamic knowledge in India before returning to lead the Azikhel — and onward through his sons Abdul Manan and Abdul Hayi, to Inam Ullah, and to Shafi Khan.

زموږ کورنۍ د اکوزي له لارې، او په اکوزي کې د بایزي له لارې راځي. زموږ خپله څانګه، کمال خیل، د کمال خان له نامه اخیستل شوې، چې قبر یې لا هم په شنګله، بشام کیرۍ کې دی.

Present Day

Scattered, But Connected

In more recent generations, members of our family have moved beyond Shangla — to Peshawar, to cities across Pakistan, and to countries like Saudi Arabia and Qatar — in search of education and better opportunities. This site exists because of that distance: to make sure no member of our family, wherever they are, ever loses their connection to where they come from.

وروستیو نسلونو کې، زموږ د کورنۍ غړي له شنګلې بهر — پیښور، نورو ښارونو، او هیوادونو ته لاړل. دا ویب پاڼه همدې لپاره جوړه شوې چې هیڅوک دې خپل اصل هیرول نشي.

A Note

This history is compiled from general historical accounts of the Yousafzai tribe alongside our own family's confirmed genealogy. Broader tribal history is well documented; the more specific details of our own branch beyond Shafi Khan are still being verified with our elders and will be corrected and expanded as we confirm more.
