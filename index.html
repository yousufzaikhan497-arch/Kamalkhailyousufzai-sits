Our story / تاریخ
Our family's story — where we come from, the ancestors we're descended from, and how we ended up spread so far apart. Elders are welcome to correct or add to this.

Origins

We belong to the Kamal Khel branch of the Yousafzai, one of the largest Pashtun tribes, tracing descent through Yousaf down to the wider Pashtun genealogical line. Like the rest of the Yousafzai, our ancestors moved out of the Kandahar region generations ago and settled across the Peshawar and Swat valleys, eventually spreading into the districts that make up Khyber Pakhtunkhwa today — including our own homeland, Shangla.

موږ د یوسفزو د لوی پښتون قبیلې کمال خیلو څانګه یو. زموږ نیکونه له قندهار څخه کډه شوي او د پیښور او سوات په درو کې میشته شوي، او وروسته یې خپله سیمه، شنګلی، ونیوله.

Notable ancestors

Our lineage runs from Khan Usman, through his son Charabi Khan, to Kamal Khan — the ancestor our branch takes its name from. From Kamal Khan the line continues through Muladaray Khan, Mohammed Salim, and Mohammed Aurang, down to Samiullah Yousafzai, a respected leader born in the early 1800s who studied Islamic knowledge in India before returning home to lead the Azikhel with a large following. He is remembered as a defender of his community, and his tomb still stands in Besham, Butyal, in Shangla.

زموږ شجره له خان عثمان، چارابي خان، کمال خان (چې زموږ څانګه ورڅخه نومول شوې)، مولادراۍ خان، محمد سلیم، محمد اورنګ، تر ثمیع الله یوسفزي پورې رسیږي، چې د خپلې ټولنې مدافع ګڼل کیده.

How we became scattered

Over generations, the wider Yousafzai spread naturally across many districts — Swat, Buner, Mardan, Swabi, Malakand, Shangla, and beyond, as well as parts of Afghanistan. More recently, our own family has scattered further still: for education, work, and better opportunities, members moved to cities like Peshawar and abroad. That distance is exactly why this website exists — to bring us back into one place, even when we live apart.

د زمانو په تیریدو سره، یوسفزي په ډیرو سیمو کې خپاره شول. زموږ خپله کورنۍ هم د زده کړې او کار لپاره نورو ښارونو او هیوادونو ته لاړه. همدا واټن دی چې دا ویب پاڼه یې لپاره جوړه شوې.
